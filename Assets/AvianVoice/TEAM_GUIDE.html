Birdflow / Avian Voice

Team handbook · live lab as of October 2026

VR research prototype for a bird-body illusion. Voice is the only gameplay input. Open Assets/AvianVoice/Scenes/Avian_Lab.unity.

1. What this project is

Birdflow is a Unity 6 VR prototype. The playable module is Avian Voice under Assets/AvianVoice/. It supports CHI 2027 work on:

  • Bird body illusion — first-person parrot, chest, wings, beak, pond reflection, baroque mirror.
  • Voice as the only gameplay input — mic drives song, flight climb, and pitch matching. There is no Space-key breath.
  • Guided embodiment — a teacher timeline moves the player bird’s chest. The companion parrot appears when the task is singing together.

Player bird: PlayerParrot. Companion: FriendParrot (hidden except in Singing). RoleMarker spheres are not gameplay.

2. How to run

EngineUnity 6000.3.4f1 (Unity 6), URP
XROpenXR, XR Interaction Toolkit, XR Hands
SettingsAssets/AvianVoice/Settings/AvianVoiceSettings.asset
  1. Open the lab scene and press Play.
  2. Allow the microphone. On Windows, capture starts, the audio device is reset so speakers work, then capture restarts once.
  3. Pick a scene with the I want … chip, M, or the XR menu button. Keys 1–6 also switch. C cheats (turn off enableDebugCheat before a study).

Desktop: right-mouse look, WASD move (WASD off during Flying). E / Q nudge altitude. Debug HUD stays off while showHudPanels is false; the wish menu and scene prompt stay visible.

4. Six scenes — task and user flow

All six live inside Avian_Lab. TaskManager enables one behaviour at a time.

1 Meet (meet) — “I want to meet my bird self.” Look at the player bird in the mirror. Friend hidden. Completes after ~6 seconds. Script: MeetBirdTask.cs.

2 Breath Sync (breath_sync) — “I want to learn its breath.” Watch the player bird breathe on a teacher timeline (Pattern A 4/4 × 3 cycles, then Pattern B 6/6 × 2). No breath key. Scripts: BreathSyncTask.cs, BirdFriendDemonstrator.cs, BreathSimulator.cs.

3 Flying (flying) — “I want to fly above the water.” Auto-glide forward. Sing louder or higher to climb, stay quiet to descend. Pond reflection on; player mesh hidden from the headset. Scripts: FlightControlTask.cs, ParrotFlightPresentation.cs.

4 Singing (singing) — “I want to sing as a bird.” Friend visible. Companion plays bird_melody.wav. Hold 330 → 392 → 440 → 494 Hz (~40 Hz, 0.45 s). Script: SingingBranchTask.cs.

5 Call Friends (call_friends) — “I want to call my bird friends.” Listen, sing back, flock arrives (1 → 3 → flock). Scripts: CallFriendsTask.cs, Embodiment/Flock/.

6 Reflection (reflection) — “I want to rest by the water.” Mirror on, scores, farewell. Script: ReflectionTask.cs.

Intended order 1→6. The wish card can jump. Prompt strings live in TaskManager.cs.

5. How human voice becomes bird song

Microphone
  → MicrophoneCapture (restart once after AudioSettings.Reset)
  → VoiceAnalyzer (RMS + YIN)
  → BirdVoiceMapper
       Transform + synth (default): onset → BirdSongSynth.PlayChirp
       Transform, synth off: BirdCallLibrary sample chirps
       Assistive: AssistiveMonitor + mixer MicPitch / MicReverb

Pitch contour

birdHz = clamp(humanHz * synthPitchRatio, synthBirdMinHz, synthBirdMaxHz)

Default ratio 8 (three octaves). 220 Hz → 1760 Hz; 440 Hz → 3520 Hz. Intervals stay the same.

Rhythm

Not a metronome. A chirp fires on voice onset after silence, or on a loudness dip-then-rise (two quick notes). ~45 ms debounce only. synthChirpSeconds (~0.08 s) is chirp length, not the gap between notes.

Clips (not the user’s voice)

FileUse
bird_melody.wavCompanion / flock song
bird_pitch.wavFallback phrases
birds_forest.wavSuccess sting
birds_reward.wavLonger reward bed

Played 2D. After Windows speaker restore, melody is replayed if it should still be playing. Tune in AvianVoiceSettings: synthPitchRatio, voicedThreshold (0.012), yinThreshold, loudnessSmooth.

1. このプロジェクトは何か

Birdflow のプレイ可能モジュールは Avian Voice（Assets/AvianVoice/）です。CHI 2027 向けに、鳥の身体錯覚、声だけの入力、教師タイムラインによる胸部、歌うときだけ相棒オウム、を扱います。

プレイヤーは PlayerParrot。相棒は FriendParrot（歌う場面以外は非表示）。RoleMarker の球はプレイ要素ではありません。

2. 起動方法

Unity 6000.3.4f1 / URP / OpenXR。ラボシーンを Play。マイク許可。Windows ではスピーカー復帰のためオーディオを Reset し、キャプチャを一度やり直します。I want … / M / キー 1–6 で場面切替。C はチート。デスクトップは右クリック視点、WASD（飛行中は無効）、高度は E/Q。

3. 入力と出力

入力: マイク（主）、願いメニュー、1–6、C、飛行の E/Q。呼吸キーはない。BreathSimulator は教師パターンをコピーする。

出力: Transform 時は音程の輪郭を保った短い合成 chirp。体験音は Resources/BirdExperience/。鏡は 1/2/4/6、池の反射は 3/5。ログは avian_trials.jsonl。

4. 6 つの場面

1 Meet — 鏡の自分を見る。呼吸課題なし。約 6 秒。

2 Breath Sync — 教師タイムラインでプレイヤー鳥が呼吸。A（4/4×3）のあと B（6/6×2）。

3 Flying — 自動滑空。声で上昇、沈黙で降下。池に映る。本体はヘッドセットから隠す。

4 Singing — 相棒が bird_melody.wav を歌う。330→392→440→494 Hz を保持。

5 Call Friends — メロディを歌い返し、1→3→群れ。

6 Reflection — 水辺で休み、スコア。

推奨順は 1→6。願いカードで飛ばしてよい。文言は TaskManager.cs と SceneWishMenu.cs。

5. 人の声 → 鳥の声

マイク → MicrophoneCapture → VoiceAnalyzer (YIN)
  → BirdVoiceMapper → BirdSongSynth.PlayChirp

音程: birdHz = clamp(humanHz × 8, 1600, 5500)。メロディの形はそのまま。

リズム: 固定拍子ではない。無音後の起音、または音量の谷→山で次の一声。デバウンス約 45 ms。synthChirpSeconds は一声の長さ（約 0.08 秒）。

bird_melody.wav が相棒の歌。birds_forest.wav が成功音。2D 再生。

1. 项目 2. 运行 3. 输入输出 4. 六个场景 5. 人声→鸟鸣 6. 代码 7. 图层

1. 项目是什么

Birdflow 的可玩模块是 Avian Voice（Assets/AvianVoice/），服务 CHI 2027 相关工作：鸟身幻觉、嗓音作为唯一玩法输入、教师时间轴驱动胸部。没有 Space 呼吸键。伙伴鹦鹉只在唱歌场景出现。

玩家：PlayerParrot。伙伴：FriendParrot。不要把 RoleMarker 小球当玩法。

2. 怎么运行

Unity 6000.3.4f1 / URP / OpenXR。Play 实验室场景，允许麦克风。Windows 会 Reset 音频设备以恢复扬声器，再重启一次采集。用 I want … / M / 数字键 1–6 换场景。C 作弊。桌面右键环顾，WASD（飞行时关闭），高度 E/Q。showHudPanels 为 false 时不显示调试 HUD，许愿菜单仍在。

3. 输入与输出

输入：麦克风（主）、许愿菜单、1–6、C、飞行 E/Q。没有呼吸按键。BreathSimulator 只复制教师节奏。

输出：默认 Transform 为跟音高轮廓走的短促合成鸟鸣。体验音频在 Resources/BirdExperience/。镜子：1/2/4/6。池塘反射：3/5。日志：avian_trials.jsonl。

4. 六个场景与 user flow

1 遇见（meet）— 看镜子里的自己，不必呼吸，约 6 秒。MeetBirdTask.cs

2 呼吸（breath_sync）— 看玩家鸟按教师节奏呼吸。A（4/4×3）再 B（6/6×2）。BreathSyncTask.cs

3 飞行（flying）— 自动向前。大声或更高则升，安静则降。水面倒影；本体对头显隐藏。FlightControlTask.cs、ParrotFlightPresentation.cs

4 唱歌（singing）— 伙伴播 bird_melody.wav。保持 330→392→440→494 Hz。SingingBranchTask.cs

5 召朋友（call_friends）— 听旋律唱回去，1→3→鸟群。CallFriendsTask.cs

6 回望（reflection）— 水边休息，看分数。ReflectionTask.cs

推荐顺序 1→6，也可用许愿卡跳转。文案在 TaskManager.cs 与 SceneWishMenu.cs。

5. 人声如何变成鸟叫

麦克风 → MicrophoneCapture → VoiceAnalyzer（YIN）
  → BirdVoiceMapper → BirdSongSynth.PlayChirp

音调：鸟鸣 Hz = clamp(人声 Hz × 8, 1600, 5500)。音程关系不变，整体抬到鸟的频段。

节奏：不是节拍器。从静音起音，或响度先落再升（连唱两下）时立刻叫。约 45 ms 防抖。synthChirpSeconds 是一声的时长（约 0.08 秒）。

伙伴的歌是 bird_melody.wav。成功提示 birds_forest.wav。全部 2D。调参在 AvianVoiceSettings。
